# Asset Licenses

## Built-in sound packs

The four built-in packs (`thock`, `clicky`, `linear`, `soft`) and their WAV
files are generated deterministically from mathematical synthesis by
`scripts/generate_builtin_packs.py`. They contain no third-party recordings.

The generated audio files and manifests are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may use, modify, and redistribute them without attribution. Attribution to
TactileMac is appreciated but not required.

## App icon

The app icon is original vector/raster artwork generated by
`scripts/generate_app_icon.py`. It is released under CC0-1.0 with the rest of
the generated assets.

## Test fixtures

`TactileMacTests/Fixtures` contains only tones and clicks generated by
`scripts/generate_fixtures.py`. The Ogg Vorbis fixture is encoded from that
generated WAV. Fixture audio is CC0-1.0.

No third-party audio or image assets are bundled.
